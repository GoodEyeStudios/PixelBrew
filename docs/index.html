What it does

Pick a source, point it at the panel, hit Start. Queue several sources to rotate on timers with a live countdown. Minimizes to the tray and gets out of your way.

📊 System monitorCPU · GPU · disk · network graphs, configurable.
🎵 Audio spectrumLive analyzer from any audio device.
🎬 Video & YouTubeLocal files, YouTube, RTSP/HLS streams.
🖥️ Screen & windowMirror a region or capture a window.
📱 Cast to itDLNA target — cast from a phone on your LAN.
🕒 Clock · ⛅ WeatherPlus slideshow and a test/calibration card.
🎨 Themes8 built-in, plus your own JSON palettes.
🔁 Smart queueLoop modes, per-item timers, tray.

Free & donationware. No license key, no nag screens. Needs Windows 10/11 and the panel; close the stock vendor app first — it holds the panel open.
